TURBO PITCH — a single-file car-soccer game with Rocket-League-accurate physics.
Original title, cars, arena, sounds and UI; all assets are generated in code.
Only external dependency: Three.js r186 (ES module from jsDelivr via the import map).

CONTROLS (all remappable in Settings > Bindings; saved in localStorage)
  Keyboard & mouse            Gamepad (standard mapping)
  W / S   throttle / reverse   RT / LT   throttle / reverse
  A / D   steer, air yaw        Left stick  steer / pitch / yaw
  Arrows  air pitch / yaw       A          jump
  Right mouse  jump             B          boost
  Left mouse   boost            X          powerslide / air roll
  Left Shift   powerslide / free air roll (A/D roll while held)
  Q / E   air roll left/right   LB / RB    air roll left / right
  Space   ball cam              Y          ball cam
  Tab     scoreboard            Back/View  scoreboard
  R       reset ball (free play) D-pad up  reset ball (free play)
  Esc     pause                 Start      pause
  F3      debug overlay + physics self-tests
  Right stick / mouse (when enabled): free look

PHYSICS SOURCES
  * RocketSim by ZealanL (github.com/ZealanL/RocketSim, MIT License, (c) 2022 ZealanL):
    RLConst.h, Car.cpp, CarConfig.cpp, Ball.cpp, Arena.cpp, BoostPad.cpp and btVehicleRL.cpp
    were ported to JavaScript line by line (constants, curves, suspension, wheel friction
    impulses, sticky forces, jump/dodge/air control, auto-flip/roll, boost, supersonic,
    extra car->ball impulse, bump/demo rules, pad pickup volumes, kickoff/respawn spawns).
  * Bullet 3.24 as modified by RocketSim: sequential-impulse contact solver (10 iterations,
    split impulse with erp2 0.8, restitution threshold 0.2 bt/s, static friction=min /
    restitution=max combine rules).
  * RLBot / RLGym "useful game values" tables (arena dimensions, pad and spawn positions).
  Units: Unreal Units (1 uu = 1 cm), Z-up, fixed 120 Hz; render converts to Three.js (Y-up, m).

KEY CONSTANTS (see src/sim/const.js in the repository)
  gravity -650; ball r 91.25, mass 30, drag 0.03, max 6000 uu/s, world friction/restitution
  0.35/0.6; car mass 180, max 2300 uu/s, 5.5 rad/s; supersonic 2200 (2100 maintain, 1 s);
  boost 33.33/s, accel 991.67 ground / 1058.33 air; throttle 1600 uu/s^2 to 1410;
  brake 3500, coast 525; jump 291.67 + 1458.33 hold (0.025-0.2 s); dodge 500 (2.5x back,
  1.9x side); flip torque 260/224 for 0.65 s; air torque (130,95,400)*2pi/65536*1000.
